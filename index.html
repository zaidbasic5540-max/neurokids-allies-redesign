About Us

Fostering Reconciliation, Belonging & Inclusion

Addressing the root causes of misunderstanding across all environments where children grow.

NeuroKids Allies creates empathy-centered resources that equip children, individuals, faith leaders, and communities to better understand, accept, and support their neurodivergent loved ones.

Our work addresses neuro-bias, isolation, and community segregation, thereby reducing fractured relationships between neurotypes and fostering reconciliation, belonging, and inclusion across the environments that impact a neurodivergent child’s development.

Root Misconceptions

Neuro Bias

Misunderstanding, exclusion, and demeaning assumptions about neurodivergent children have led to broken relationships, isolation, and unresolved trauma for neurodivergent families.

Emotional Impact

Isolation

Children grow up internalizing exclusion and shame inherited from stigmatizing experiences. Families withdraw from gatherings and relationships, knowing their differently abled children will be stigmatized.

Community Fracture

Community Segregation

Communities and faith groups lose families who no longer feel welcome.

About The Founder

Rooted in Lived Experience & Lifelong Advocacy

“From neuro assumptions to understanding, and from inclusion as a policy to the lived experience for each neurodivergent child.”

Omotayo Senbore is the founder of NeuroKids Allies. Her work is grounded in decades of lived experience as a neurodivergent mother raising a neurodivergent child, navigating systems and spaces not designed with neurodivergent families in mind.

She creates neuro-affirming empathy-based resources, empowering children, adults, communities and faith leaders to create affirming spaces where neurodivergent children are accepted as they are.

Omotayo's mission is to help individuals and communities move from neuro assumptions to understanding and from inclusion as a policy to the lived experience for each neurodivergent child.
